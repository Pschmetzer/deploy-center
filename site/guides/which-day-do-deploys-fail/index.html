When do your deploys fail most often?

deploys when shows the failure rate of your deploys per weekday or per hour of the day (UTC), from the journal. A slot needs 5 deploys to count, so a hint is never based on one bad afternoon.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · by Patrick Schmetzer, author of Deploy Center

Deutsche Fassung

1. Run it

deploys when                      # by weekday, last 90 days
deploys when --by hour --days 180
deploys when --json

A made-up example with too little data:

Failure rate by weekday, last 90 days (UTC)
  Sat  50%     1 of 2  (too few to say)
  Sun  50%     1 of 2  (too few to say)
  No slot stands out.

2. Read it with care

  • A slot with fewer than 5 deploys is marked too few to say.
  • The times are UTC. Shift them to your zone before you pick a time.
  • It is a hint from your own history, not a rule. A bad Friday can come from what you shipped on Fridays, not from Friday.

3. Turn it into a rule

If one slot stands out, you can close it on purpose. A freeze is a rule in window.json:

deploys freeze add fri 15:00 --reason "Friday afternoon fails more often"
deploys freeze status

Inside a closed window a deploy needs --force "reason" and the reason goes to the journal. More in the freeze guide. Every number comes from the journal on your Mac. No network, no platform call, nothing is started.

Questions

Why does it say too few to say?

A weekday or an hour needs at least 5 deploys in the period before it counts. Use --days 180 or look at another grouping.

Does it set a freeze for me?

No. It only shows numbers. You set a freeze yourself with deploys freeze add.

Is the failure rate per project?

It adds up all deploys in the journal for the period. Look at one project with deploys streak --project web or deploys timeline --project web.

Which time zone is used?

UTC, for weekdays and hours.
