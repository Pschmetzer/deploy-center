How to roll back a bad deploy on Vercel or Render

On Vercel open Deployments, pick the last good production deployment and choose Instant Rollback, or run vercel rollback. On Render open the service, find the last good deploy and roll back to it. Then check the site.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · by Patrick Schmetzer, author of Deploy Center

A rollback points production at a deployment that already built, so nothing is rebuilt and it is fast. It does not undo database migrations or changes to environment variables.

Vercel

  1. Open the project, then Deployments.
  2. Pick the last production deployment that worked and use Instant Rollback from its menu.

From a terminal, vercel rollback does the same. After a rollback Vercel may stop assigning new production deployments to your domains automatically until you promote one again, so read what the dashboard says before you push the fix.

Render

  1. Open the service in the Render dashboard.
  2. Find the last deploy that worked and roll back to it.

Render also has a rollback call in its API. With auto-deploy on, the next push deploys again, so turn it off, or fix forward, before you rely on the rollback holding.

Check the site afterwards

A rollback that you did not test is a guess. Ask the site, not the dashboard: the home page, a path that touches your database, and your health route.

Rollback in Deploy Center

Deploy Center has a Rollback button in the app, and deploys vercel rollback <project> and deploys render rollback <project> in the CLI, both going back to the deploy before the live one. deploys verify <project> --path /health asks the site after a deploy and rolls back on a 5xx by itself; --no-rollback only reports. See safe deploys.

Questions

Does a rollback rebuild my app?

No. It points production at a deployment that already built, so it is fast.

Does a rollback undo a database migration?

No. Migrations and environment variable changes stay as they are.

Can a push undo my rollback?

On Render with auto-deploy on, the next push deploys again. On Vercel, check the dashboard after a rollback: production domains may need a promotion before new deployments are assigned again.

Can Deploy Center roll back by itself?

Yes. deploys verify asks the site after a deploy and rolls back on a 5xx unless you pass --no-rollback.
